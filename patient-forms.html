Patient Forms

Please complete the following forms before your visit. You can download and fill them out at home, or arrive early to complete them in our office.

New Patient Registration Form

Required for all new patients. Includes contact information, medical history, and emergency contacts.

Vision & Eye Health History

Detailed information about your vision problems, previous eye conditions, and family eye health history.

Medical History Form

General health information including current medications, allergies, and any medical conditions that may affect your vision.

Contact Lens Fitting Agreement

Required for patients interested in contact lenses. Includes fitting guidelines and care instructions.

Insurance Information Form

Please provide your insurance information to help us process your claim efficiently.

HIPAA Privacy Notice

Our privacy policy regarding your protected health information and how we handle your personal data.

Dr. Fast's Qualifications

Doctor of Optometry (OD)

Earned from an accredited optometry school with advanced training in comprehensive eye care, including diagnosis and treatment of eye diseases.

Licensed Optometrist

Fully licensed to practice optometry with the authority to conduct comprehensive eye exams, prescribe eyeglasses and contact lenses, and diagnose and treat certain eye diseases.

Board Certified

Maintains current certification and continuing education in all areas of optometry practice, ensuring the highest standards of patient care.

Specialty Training

Advanced training in specialty areas including contact lens fitting, pediatric eye care, and management of eye diseases such as glaucoma and diabetic retinopathy.

Professional Memberships

Active member of professional optometry organizations committed to advancing the profession and ensuring patient safety and satisfaction.

Years of Experience

Extensive experience providing comprehensive eye care to patients of all ages with a focus on personalized, compassionate care.

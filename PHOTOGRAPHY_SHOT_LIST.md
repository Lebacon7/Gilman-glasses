## Overview

This shot list provides a comprehensive guide for the photography shoot. All images should maintain professional quality suitable for a medical/optometry practice website. Focus on clean, well-lit shots with proper exposure and composition.

**Technical Requirements:**
- Resolution: Minimum 2000x1500px (horizontal orientation preferred for most shots)
- Format: JPG or PNG, RGB color space
- Lighting: Professional/natural lighting to showcase the practice environment
- No watermarks or logos unless specified
- Deliver high-resolution files; website will optimize

## 8. Special Requirements

### 8.1 Image Organization & Delivery
- **Folder Structure:**
  - `/portraits` - Dr. Fast and team member headshots
  - `/team` - Individual team member headshots
  - `/facilities` - Interior and exterior facility shots
  - `/action` - Eye exams, consultations, patient care in progress
  - `/products` - Glasses, contacts, equipment close-ups
  - `/lifestyle` - Diverse patient demographics and candid shots
  - `/environment` - Exterior and accessibility shots
  - `/extra` - Alternative shots or additional variations

### 8.2 File Naming Convention
- Format: `category_subject_variation.jpg`
- Examples:
  - `portraits_dr-fast_headshot-direct.jpg`
  - `team_receptionist_headshot.jpg`
  - `facilities_reception-area_angle1.jpg`
  - `action_eye-exam_process.jpg`
  - `products_frames_modern-style.jpg`
  - `lifestyle_family_children.jpg`

### 8.3 Release Forms & Permissions
- **Required for:**
  - Any recognizable people in photos (staff, patients, models)
  - Building/facility exterior if distinctive

- **Provide:**
  - Model release forms for all people in images
  - Property release if needed for facility exteriors
  - Signed consent for staff photos

### 8.4 Additional Notes
- **Color Accuracy:** Ensure colors are accurate for frames, equipment, and signage
- **Focus & Sharpness:** All images must be sharp and in focus
- **Composition:** Follow rule of thirds for balanced, professional composition

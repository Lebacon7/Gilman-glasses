Comprehensive Eye Exams

Early detection of eye health problems is key to maintaining good vision throughout your life. Our comprehensive eye exams include state-of-the-art diagnostic testing to assess your vision and eye health.

Visual Acuity Testing

Measure how clearly you can see at various distances to determine if you need vision correction.

Refraction Assessment

Precise measurement to determine your exact prescription for glasses or contact lenses.

Eye Pressure Testing

Screen for glaucoma by measuring the pressure inside your eyes using advanced equipment.

Retinal Examination

Detailed inspection of the retina to check for any signs of disease or abnormality.

Visual Field Testing

Evaluate your peripheral vision to detect any blind spots or vision loss.

Eye Health Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation of overall eye health including screening for cataracts and other conditions.

When Should You Get an Eye Exam?

  • • Adults with no vision problems: Every 1-2 years
  • • Adults over 60: Every year
  • • Children: At least once before age 3, then before school age, then every 1-2 years
  • • People with eye conditions: As recommended by Dr. Fast

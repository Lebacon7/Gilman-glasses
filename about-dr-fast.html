About Dr. Fast

Professional Excellence in Optometry

Dr. Fast is a dedicated optometrist with extensive experience in comprehensive eye care. With a passion for helping patients achieve and maintain healthy vision, Dr. Fast has built a reputation for providing personalized care and attention to every patient who walks through our doors.

Our Commitment

At C Fast Optometry, we believe that quality eye care should be accessible and affordable. We use the latest technology and techniques to provide accurate diagnoses and effective treatments for a wide range of eye conditions.

We take time to listen to our patients' concerns and develop treatment plans that work best for their individual needs. Whether you're here for a routine eye exam or specialty care, you can expect professional, compassionate service from our entire team.
